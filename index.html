01 / ABOUT THE WORKSHOP

A place to understand how things work.

Our engineering workshop gives students a safe place to practise practical skills. Students can measure materials, make small parts, test ideas, and work on class projects.

New students are welcome. Staff members are available to explain the correct way to use each tool.

03 / SAFETY RULES

Work carefully.
Stay safe.

  1. Wear safety equipment. Use safety glasses and closed shoes before starting.
  2. Check your tools. Make sure the tool and work area are in good condition.
  3. Keep the area clean. Return tools and clean your table after use.
  4. Ask when unsure. Speak to a staff member before using unfamiliar equipment.
